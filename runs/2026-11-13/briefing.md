# Trend Radar briefing - 2026-11-13

## Headline

This is run zero, the baseline.  Thirteen trends are registered across data management, AI/ML and agentic automation, with three watch-list items.  The strongest signals are semantics as core infrastructure, the gap between broad AI adoption and concentrated enterprise value, agents moving from pilots to scale amid a shake-out, and the customer's agent becoming a sales channel for airlines.

## What changed since the previous run

Not applicable.  All trends start with momentum `baseline`.  Movement will be reported from the first scheduled run.

## New on the watch list

- **W-01 Entry-level analytical work exposed.**  Gartner expects 75% of hiring processes to test AI proficiency by 2027 (Gartner, March 2026, EV-0002).  Coverage of the Stanford AI Index reports a 20% fall in entry-level developer jobs (Stanford HAI via Digit, April 2026, EV-0007).  An airline group has linked 4,000 administrative role reductions to digitalisation and AI (Lufthansa Group, September 2025, EV-0023).
- **W-02 Physical AI and world models.**  Gartner expects physical-world agents to generate ten times more data than digital AI by 2029 (Gartner, March 2026, EV-0002).  More likely relevant to operations than to commercial functions.
- **W-03 Coding agents shift build-versus-buy.**  McKinsey reports organisations building software with coding agents instead of buying it (McKinsey, August 2026, EV-0012).

## What it means for the airline commercial analyst

The evidence points to the analyst's role moving from producing answers to engineering the conditions for trustworthy machine answers.  That means owning shared commercial definitions (DM-01), specifying and evaluating agent decisions (AG-04), and making the airline's offers legible to third-party agents (AG-05).  IATA's finding that European passengers are the least likely to use AI for travel planning (35%) suggests a short window to prepare before agent-driven shopping reaches the scale seen in other regions (IATA via Nairametrics, October 2026, EV-0020).
