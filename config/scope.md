# Scope

## Domains

1. **Data management** (prefix `DM`): how enterprise data is stored, organised, governed, described and delivered, including semantics, platforms, catalogues, streaming and data governance.
2. **AI/ML** (prefix `AI`): model capability, cost, adoption, value realisation, regulation, sovereignty and trust.
3. **Agentic solutions and automation** (prefix `AG`): AI systems that plan and act, including their capability, adoption, protocols, governance, and their role as customers or channels.

Where a trend spans domains, file it under the domain where the change originates and mention the overlap in its definition.

## Lens

The audience is senior airline commercial leaders and commercial analysts.  Industry relevance is judged against pricing, revenue management, network planning, distribution, loyalty, ancillaries and commercial analytics.

## Horizon

Two to three years, with longer-range signals recorded on the watch list.

## Out of scope

- Consumer AI products, except where they change how travellers search and book.
- Hardware and chips, except where they materially change AI cost or availability.
- Customer-specific or confidential information of any kind.
